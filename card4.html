The Statue of
Akhenaten

A Revolutionary King

A limestone statue of King Akhenaten, one of the most famous pharaohs of the 18th Dynasty. He introduced new religious ideas and is known for his unique artistic style.

♛PERIOD

18th Dynasty

◇MATERIAL

Limestone

↕HEIGHT

65 cm

▦REIGN

1353–1336 BC

About This Artifact

A distinctive statue with stylized features, including the oval face, long neck, and slim physique, which reflect the artistic changes during Akhenaten's reign. It represents a major shift in Egyptian art and religion, focusing on the worship of the sun disk Aten. The statue shows the king with soft, curved lines unlike the strict style used by earlier pharaohs.
